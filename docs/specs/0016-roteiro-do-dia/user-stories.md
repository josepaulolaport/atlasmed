# Spec 0016 — User stories

Companion to [`requirements.md`](./requirements.md). Same decisions, told from the user's side.
Every story names the spec section that defines its behaviour, so a disagreement between this
document and that one is a bug in this one.

---

## Personas

**Rafael — agente comercial (REP).** Carries 145–447 clinics in the Ortopedia linha. Drives to
clinics; some days he is in one city, some days he covers four. He plans his week in his head and
in WhatsApp. He is measured on sales, and — from now on — on coverage.

**Camila — gestora (MANAGER).** Runs five reps. Today she can see what they sold and, since spec
0014, how they are performing. She cannot see what they *planned*, whether the plan was any good,
or which clinics nobody has walked into in a year.

**Ops/Admin.** Owns the catalogue and the parameters. Never plans a day.

---

## The story this feature exists to change

**Today.** Rafael wakes up, opens Explorar, filters by funnel stage, reads a few clinic screens to
remember who is worth the trip, checks the map, decides an order in his head, and types four or
five calendar events by hand. Twenty minutes, every morning, before he has driven anywhere. He
visits the clinics he remembers. Of his 445 clinics, `interactions` and `visits` hold **zero rows**
— the system has no idea which ones he has ever walked into.

**After.** Rafael opens the app. Five clinics, in order, with drive times, on a map, each one saying
why it is there:

> **1 · Instituto Cohen** — 08:30–09:15 · 12 min de deslocamento
> *24 ortopedistas registrados aqui* · *Nunca comprou* · *Ainda não visitada*

He swaps one for a clinic he knows is closed, taps **Confirmar**, and his agenda is built —
travel time included. He drives. At each stop he taps **Iniciar**, and at the end **Concluir** with
an outcome. If one of them orders, the order screen opens with the clinic already filled in.

Camila opens Equipe and sees that Rafael has covered 68 % of his book this half, that his
prospecting stops convert at 11 %, and that four clinics he keeps rejecting as *fechada* need
removing from his book.

## P1 — The ranked, explained list

> Ships without Mapbox. This alone is more than exists today.

### S1 · Ver o roteiro do dia
**As** Rafael, **I want** the app to tell me which clinics to visit today, **so that** I stop
spending the first twenty minutes of every day deciding.

- Opening **Roteiro do dia** shows up to 5 clinics, ranked, without me configuring anything.
- The limit comes from `roteiro_params.daily_limit` and never exceeds it (§6.2).
- The list is scoped to my linha and to clinics assigned to me (§4.1).
- A clinic already scheduled today, or visited inside its cooldown, does not appear (§4.1).

### S2 · Saber por que cada clínica está ali
**As** Rafael, **I want** each suggestion to say why, **so that** I can disagree with it, and so I
know what to talk about when I arrive.

- Every stop shows 2–3 reasons in plain Portuguese.
- Every reason traces to a field in `score_breakdown` (§5.2). Nothing on the card is generated by
  a language model (§12).
- Tapping **Por quê?** expands the full breakdown with each component's contribution.
- A clinic with no competitor survey says *"potencial não medido"* rather than implying we know
  (§4.2b).

### S3 · Não receber sugestões impossíveis
**As** Rafael, whose book can span several states, **I want** suggestions I can actually reach
today, **so that** the roteiro is a plan and not a wish.

- Candidates are filtered by distance from my origin before ranking (§4.1).
- If nothing is close, the radius expands and the screen **tells me how far it had to reach**.
- Origin is my GPS, or my base, or my território's centroid — and the screen says which (§6.1).

### S4 · Ser levado a clínicas que nunca visitei
**As** Camila, **I want** reps to reach clinics that have never bought and clinics that went
inactive, **so that** the whole book gets worked, not just the easy third.

- Every slate reserves one **coverage slot** drawn from clinics overdue for coverage, oldest first
  (§4.3.1).
- Default quotas are `PROSPECTAR` 3 / `MANTER` 1 / `RECUPERAR` 1 — this book is 93.8 % prospects
  (§4.3).
- The card says *"Ainda não visitada"* or *"Sem visita há 8 meses"*.
- If a bucket cannot be filled, the slate **says so and says why** (§4.8). It is never silently
  absent.

### S5 · Ajustar como a máquina pensa
**As** Ops, **I want** to change the limit, the weights and how each unit type is treated **without
a deploy**, **so that** the engine follows the commercial strategy rather than a past decision.

- `daily_limit`, weights, τ, cooldowns and coverage horizons live in `roteiro_params` (§6.3).
- `unit_type_policy` sets `fit`, `eligible` and `forceRemote` per CNES unit type (§4.2g).
- Unit types are chosen from a picker off `registry.unit_types`, never typed free-hand.
- Changing a value affects the next generation. Roteiros already generated keep the parameters they
  were built with (§5.1).

## P2 — Order, map, and a real agenda

### S6 · Ver a ordem e o tempo de deslocamento
**As** Rafael, **I want** the visiting order and the drive between each stop, **so that** I know the
day fits before I start it.

- Stops are ordered to maximise merit per hour, not to minimise distance (§4.5).
- Each stop shows the travel time from the previous one.
- The footer totals stops, driving time and the finish time.
- A day that does not fit is **shortened and reported**, never silently truncated (§4.5 step 6).

### S7 · Ver no mapa
**As** Rafael, **I want** to see the stops and the route drawn, **so that** I can sanity-check the
plan the way I always have — visually.

- Numbered pins in visiting order, plus the driving polyline and my origin (§8.1).
- `REMOTE` stops appear in a strip below the map, not as pins — nobody drives to a phone call.
- Tapping a pin scrolls to that card.

### S8 · Confirmar e ter minha agenda montada
**As** Rafael, **I want** one tap to turn the roteiro into my agenda, **so that** I never type five
calendar events again.

- **Confirmar** creates one `calendar` + one `interactions` row per stop (§7.3).
- Start times account for travel: a stop never begins before the previous one ends plus the drive.
- Confirming twice changes nothing (idempotency receipt).
- If my calendar changed since the roteiro was generated, I get the **conflicts** — the app does not
  quietly move my times (§7.3).

### S9 · Continuar funcionando quando a rede não ajuda
**As** Rafael, in a car, in a lift, in Manaus, **I want** the feature to degrade instead of failing.

- Mapbox unavailable → times are straight-line estimates, clearly labelled **estimado**, and the map
  draws lines rather than routes (§4.8).
- No GPS → base, then território centroid, always stated.

## P3 — It is my plan, not the machine's

### S10 · Trocar uma sugestão
**As** Rafael, **I want** to swap any stop, **so that** I stay in charge of my day.

- **Trocar** offers 5 alternatives ranked by what fits *that position*, each showing what it costs
  the day (`+8 min`) and its own reasons (§4.7).
- Choosing one recomputes the order and every time.
- **Remover** and **Adicionar** work the same way.

### S11 · Dizer por que recusei
**As** Camila, **I want** to know why reps reject suggestions, **so that** I can fix the data instead
of blaming the engine.

- An optional reason chip: `Muito longe` · `Já visitei` · `Não é meu cliente` · `Fechada`.
- Skippable — it never blocks the swap.
- `Fechada` raises a Não Conformidade automatically (§9).
- Rejection reasons are aggregated per rep and per território (§9).

### S12 · Decidir entre ir e ligar
**As** Rafael, **I want** the app to propose phone contacts where a drive is not worth it, **and** to
let me overrule it.

- Modality is recommended per stop, and `REMOTE` stops cost zero travel and 15 minutes (§4.4).
- Unit types can be configured to default to remote — aimed at one-surgeon consultórios (§4.2g).
- I can flip any stop; the day re-times and my choice is recorded (§10).

## P4 — Planning around what I already have

### S13 · "Já vou visitar X — o que mais?"
**As** Rafael, **I want** to anchor a visit I have already agreed and have the app fill the rest of
the day around it, **so that** a single confirmed appointment becomes a full day.

- I pick a clinic and a time; it becomes a fixed stop the engine never moves (§4.5 step 3).
- Everything else is planned around it, using its location as part of the route.
- Works with an already-scheduled interaction or an ad-hoc point.

---

## P5 — Closing the loop

### S14 · Registrar o que aconteceu
**As** Rafael, **I want** finishing a visit to take one tap, **so that** recording the outcome is not
a chore I do on Friday from memory.

- **Iniciar** on arrival, **Concluir** at the end (existing interaction lifecycle).
- Concluir asks for an outcome: `Pedido` · `Promessa` · `Sem interesse` · `Não encontrou` ·
  `Reagendar` (§5.3).
- `Pedido` opens the order flow with the clinic prefilled.
- The outcome is stored on `visits`, which today holds a timestamp and nothing else.

### S15 · Ver cobertura da equipe
**As** Camila, **I want** to see how much of each rep's book has actually been walked, **so that**
coverage is managed rather than hoped for.

- **Cobertura**: share of a rep's book confirmed at least once within its horizon (§4.3.1).
- **Aderência**: stops completed ÷ stops confirmed.
- **Conversão por balde**: of confirmed stops, how many produced an order within 14 days, split
  `MANTER` / `RECUPERAR` / `PROSPECTAR` (§9). This is the number that says whether the weights are
  right.

### S16 · Propor um roteiro para meu rep
**As** Camila, **I want** to build a draft for a rep, **so that** I can coach a plan instead of
describing one.

- I can generate a `DRAFT` for a rep I manage.
- **I cannot confirm it** — writing to their calendar is theirs alone (§7.3). Manager proposes, rep
  accepts.

---

## P6 — Offline

### S17 · Ver o roteiro sem sinal
**As** Rafael, between clinics, **I want** my confirmed roteiro to open with no connection.

- A confirmed roteiro is cached with its reasons, times and a static map image (§11).
- `Iniciar`, `Concluir` and outcomes queue and replay on reconnect, idempotently.
- Generating and substituting need connectivity and say so plainly.

## Not in v1, and reps will ask for these

| Ask | Where it went |
|---|---|
| "Plan my whole week" | §4.6 — deferred. One day at a time. |
| "Which city should I fly to next?" | §16.1 — trip planning, deferred. Affects reps 5 and 6. |
| "Let me just ask it questions" | §12 — free chat over the CRM is out. Phase 2 is natural language that only fills the generation form; phase 3 is a grounded pre-visit briefing. |
| "Reschedule my day when a visit runs long" | Out of scope. The rep regenerates; we do not move their calendar for them. |

---

## Definition of done, per phase

A phase is done when its stories are demonstrable **on real data in the app**, and when §10's
measurement for that phase is recorded. A phase that ships without its instrumentation is a phase
whose effect nobody can argue about later — and the weights in §4.2 are guesses until that argument
is possible.
